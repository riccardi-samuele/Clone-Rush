# Clone Rush covers — ImageGen prompts

All three source PNGs in `originals/` were generated with the built-in
`image_gen` tool on 12 October 2026. Existing Clone Rush covers and a gameplay
screenshot were provided as style and product references. `exports/` contains
centered, high-quality resizes at the portal's exact dimensions. The logo in
each generated source was checked visually for the exact words **CLONE RUSH**.

## Landscape

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: landscape portal cover artwork
> for the existing game Clone Rush. Edit target: the existing landscape cover;
> supporting reference: an actual gameplay screenshot. Preserve the Candy Sky
> art direction and exact large, legible two-line title “CLONE RUSH”. Recompose
> the lower scene around two glossy square clones, one white and one pink,
> jumping in sync across separate floating candy islands, clearing three silver
> spikes and aiming for one glowing cyan portal. Use matching jump arcs, clear
> silhouettes and a playful polished 3D game-key-art finish. No purple arrow
> character, extra characters, buttons, badges, other words, store marks or
> borders. Landscape 16:9 promotional illustration, not a gameplay screenshot.

## Portrait

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: portrait 2:3 cover for Clone
> Rush. Edit target: the existing portrait cover; supporting reference: the new
> landscape cover. Preserve the exact two-line “CLONE RUSH” logo at top. Below
> it, show the white and pink square clones jumping together over three silver
> spikes toward a cyan portal on a second floating candy island. Keep the
> established vivid blue sky, clouds, green scalloped islands with blue cloud
> undersides, pink-white stripes, flowers and distant waterfall islands. Make
> a true portrait composition with safe edges and no extra text or marks.

## Square

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: square 1:1 cover for Clone
> Rush. Edit target: the existing square cover; supporting references: the new
> landscape and portrait covers. Keep the exact two-line title large and crisp
> at top. Below it, show the white and pink square clones jumping in sync over
> three silver spikes between striped floating islands toward a cyan portal.
> Each clone has exactly two dark eyes and no limbs. Keep objects visually
> separated at thumbnail scale. No unrelated collectibles, additional words,
> buttons, borders or store marks.
